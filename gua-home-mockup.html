Global University Academy

Re-imagining higher education for refugees

The Global University Academy is a collaborative initiative uniting universities and partners worldwide to expand access to higher education for refugees, displaced learners, and host communities. By pooling expertise and resources, we are working to deliver innovative, scalable, and internationally recognised learning opportunities that support durable solutions to displacement.

Where we teach

Our partner universities deliver courses directly in refugee camps and host communities. Explore the map to see where.

Our mission

The Global University Academy's mission is to empower refugees and displaced people by increasing their access to quality higher education.
